## Step: accept

*The referee did this step itself, and its check did not pass — its verdict is at the end.
You are here to fix what it found, then check again.*

The referee writes the **Acceptance** section from its own records: for each criterion, its
tests, the proof they failed before the fix (or, for a guard, passed before it), the passing
run at the reviewed commit, the suites at integrate and the reviewer's verdict.

If something can only be checked by hand, add a line `**Checked by hand:**` at the end of the
Acceptance section with what was checked and how. The referee keeps it. Do not write the
table yourself.
